# IELTS Writing Task 1 自测答案

同一题的第一种写法为首选答案；斜线后的表达为自然替换。答案不要求逐字一致，但介词、时态、单复数和数据关系必须准确。

# 第一章　通用结构与概述

## 中译英表达答案

1. **P1-STR-E-001**　compare ... in terms of ... / compare differences in ...
2. **P1-STR-E-002**　provide information about ... / present information on ...
3. **P1-STR-E-003**　illustrate changes in ... from A to B / show how ... changed between A and B
4. **P1-STR-E-004**　over the period from A to B / between A and B
5. **P1-STR-E-005**　Overall, ... / On the whole, ...
6. **P1-STR-E-006**　Looking at the details, ... / Turning to the details, ...
7. **P1-STR-E-007**　In terms of ... / Regarding ... / As for ...
8. **P1-STR-E-008**　By contrast, ... / By comparison, ... / In comparison, ...
9. **P1-STR-E-009**　Meanwhile, ... / At the same time, ...
10. **P1-STR-E-010**　follow a broadly similar pattern / show a similar pattern overall
11. **P1-STR-E-011**　show a markedly different pattern / follow a distinctly different pattern
12. **P1-STR-E-012**　throughout the period / over the whole period
13. **P1-STR-E-013**　by the end of the period / at the end of the period
14. **P1-STR-E-014**　for the remainder of the period / throughout the rest of the period
15. **P1-STR-E-015**　in chronological order of opening / according to their opening dates

## 中译英完整句答案

1. **P1-STR-S-001**　The line graph compares employment in four sectors of the US economy over the period from 1960 to 2020.
2. **P1-STR-S-002**　The table compares six underground railway systems in terms of their opening year, route length and annual passenger numbers.
3. **P1-STR-S-003**　Overall, employment in retail and healthcare increased substantially, whereas agricultural employment declined.
4. **P1-STR-S-004**　Overall, A remained the largest category throughout, while B accounted for the smallest proportion.
5. **P1-STR-S-005**　Overall, the two lower-income groups rose initially before declining slightly, while the highest-income group followed the opposite trend.
6. **P1-STR-S-006**　Looking at the details, A rose from approximately 6 million to 16 million.
7. **P1-STR-S-007**　Regarding B, the pattern was somewhat different. / As for B, a different pattern can be observed.
8. **P1-STR-S-008**　Specific figures should not be included in the overall summary. / The overview should not contain specific data.
9. **P1-STR-S-009**　The older underground railway systems tend to have longer routes and substantially higher passenger numbers.
10. **P1-STR-S-010**　The chart shows monthly changes in prices rather than the actual prices of the metals.

### 易错提醒

- 图表“展示”优先使用 `show / illustrate / compare`，不要把 `provide` 和 `show` 连用。
- `information` 和 `employment` 通常不可数。
- Overview 概括最大特征，不写具体年份和数字。

# 第二章　动态图：趋势、程度与时间

## 中译英表达答案

1. **P1-DYN-E-001**　rise from X to Y / increase from X to Y
2. **P1-DYN-E-002**　rise by X / increase by X
3. **P1-DYN-E-003**　rise steadily / show steady growth
4. **P1-DYN-E-004**　rise sharply / increase dramatically
5. **P1-DYN-E-005**　edge up / rise slightly
6. **P1-DYN-E-006**　show an upward trend / follow an upward trend
7. **P1-DYN-E-007**　fall from X to Y / decline from X to Y
8. **P1-DYN-E-008**　fall by X / decrease by X
9. **P1-DYN-E-009**　decline steadily / undergo a sustained decline
10. **P1-DYN-E-010**　fall sharply / drop dramatically
11. **P1-DYN-E-011**　decline further to ... / fall further to ...
12. **P1-DYN-E-012**　remain relatively stable / stay broadly stable
13. **P1-DYN-E-013**　change very little / remain almost unchanged
14. **P1-DYN-E-014**　level off at ... / stabilise at ...
15. **P1-DYN-E-015**　fluctuate between X and Y / show fluctuations between X and Y
16. **P1-DYN-E-016**　experience considerable fluctuations / fluctuate considerably
17. **P1-DYN-E-017**　reach a peak of X / peak at X
18. **P1-DYN-E-018**　fall to a low of X / reach its lowest point at X
19. **P1-DYN-E-019**　rise to X before declining to Y / climb to X and then fall to Y
20. **P1-DYN-E-020**　grow at a slower rate / increase more gradually
21. **P1-DYN-E-021**　experience the fastest growth / record the most rapid increase
22. **P1-DYN-E-022**　intersect at X / converge at X
23. **P1-DYN-E-023**　end the period at Y / finish at Y
24. **P1-DYN-E-024**　return to positive growth / become positive again
25. **P1-DYN-E-025**　compared with the previous month / relative to the previous month

## 中译英完整句答案

1. **P1-DYN-S-001**　Retail employment rose steadily from approximately 6 million in 1960 to around 16 million in 2020.
2. **P1-DYN-S-002**　Manufacturing employment initially rose to a peak of 20 million before undergoing a sustained decline.
3. **P1-DYN-S-003**　Agricultural employment remained stable between 1980 and 2000, before declining further to approximately 2 million.
4. **P1-DYN-S-004**　The figure remained between 0.5% and 1.5% for most of the year.
5. **P1-DYN-S-005**　Nickel experienced the greatest fluctuations, whereas copper was the most stable of the three metals.
6. **P1-DYN-S-006**　Both A and B fell below zero in June and returned to positive growth in November.
7. **P1-DYN-S-007**　Zinc recorded its largest monthly increase of the year in February, at 3%.
8. **P1-DYN-S-008**　The rate of increase gradually declined to 0.5% and remained at this level for two consecutive months.
9. **P1-DYN-S-009**　Healthcare employment rose from about 2 million to 16 million, representing the largest absolute increase among the four sectors.
10. **P1-DYN-S-010**　The figure reached approximately 11 million in 2000 and rose further to 16 million by the end of the period.
11. **P1-DYN-S-011**　A grew rapidly during the first half of the period, but its rate of growth slowed thereafter.
12. **P1-DYN-S-012**　The two lines intersected in 2010, after which A remained above B.
13. **P1-DYN-S-013**　Both figures fell by nearly 2 million between 2011 and 2015.
14. **P1-DYN-S-014**　Despite several fluctuations during the period, the overall trend was upward.
15. **P1-DYN-S-015**　A was the only category to rise initially before declining steadily.

### 易错提醒

- `from X to Y` 表示起点和终点；`by X` 表示变化量。
- 过去时间的图表统一使用一般过去时；无明确过去时间的事实型图表可使用一般现在时。
- `the price rose by 3%` 与 `a 3% rise in the price` 词性不同。
- 描述“变化率”为负数时，可写 `the figure fell below zero`，不要误写成金属价格本身降到负数。

# 第三章　数据、比例、比较与静态图表

## 中译英表达答案

1. **P1-DAT-E-001**　account for 35% of the total / represent 35% of the total
2. **P1-DAT-E-002**　constitute the largest category / form the largest group
3. **P1-DAT-E-003**　account for the smallest share / make up the smallest proportion
4. **P1-DAT-E-004**　at X and Y, respectively / X and Y, respectively
5. **P1-DAT-E-005**　the corresponding figure / the corresponding figures
6. **P1-DAT-E-006**　both stand at 8% / be 8% each
7. **P1-DAT-E-007**　be equally distributed between A and B / be divided equally between A and B
8. **P1-DAT-E-008**　jointly account for the second-largest share / together make up the second-largest proportion
9. **P1-DAT-E-009**　be tied for second place / rank joint second
10. **P1-DAT-E-010**　top the list, followed by B / rank first, with B in second place
11. **P1-DAT-E-011**　remain well below B / be considerably lower than B
12. **P1-DAT-E-012**　be slightly above / below ... / be marginally higher / lower than ...
13. **P1-DAT-E-013**　be approximately twice as high as B / be roughly double the figure for B
14. **P1-DAT-E-014**　be half that of B / be half the figure for B
15. **P1-DAT-E-015**　be at least twice the figure for the next closest category / be more than double that of the second-ranked category
16. **P1-DAT-E-016**　fall to half of its previous level / decline to around half its original level
17. **P1-DAT-E-017**　fall by approximately two-thirds / decrease by roughly two-thirds
18. **P1-DAT-E-018**　the largest absolute increase / the greatest increase in absolute terms

20. **P1-DAT-E-020**　differ substantially / vary considerably

25. **P1-DAT-E-025**　the third-longest network / rank third in terms of route length

## 中译英完整句答案

1. **P1-DAT-S-001**　Spending on food accounted for the largest proportion of families' weekly income in 1968, at 35%.
2. **P1-DAT-S-002**　Housing, clothing and footwear jointly accounted for the second-largest shares, at 10% each.
3. **P1-DAT-S-003**　The proportions of sodium consumed at breakfast and in snacks were both 14%, while the corresponding figures for saturated fat were 16% and 21%, respectively.
4. **P1-DAT-S-004**　Dinner accounted for the highest proportions of sodium and saturated fat, at 43% and 37%, respectively.
5. **P1-DAT-S-005**　By contrast, added sugar was much more heavily concentrated in snacks, which accounted for 42% of the total.
6. **P1-DAT-S-006**　Breakfast accounted for relatively low and similar proportions of all three nutrients, ranging from 14% to 16%.
7. **P1-DAT-S-007**　The two middle-income groups changed very little throughout the period.
8. **P1-DAT-S-008**　In 2015, the number of households earning more than $100,000 reached approximately 33 million, making this the largest category again.
9. **P1-DAT-S-009**　Households earning between $25,000 and $49,999 formed the largest group in 2011.
10. **P1-DAT-S-010**　London has the oldest and longest underground railway network, while Tokyo carries the largest number of passengers annually.
11. **P1-DAT-S-011**　Although Tokyo has only the third-longest network, it carries the most passengers of the six cities each year.
12. **P1-DAT-S-012**　Although the Los Angeles network is more than twice as long as Kyoto's, it carries only 5 million more passengers annually.
13. **P1-DAT-S-013**　The six underground railway systems differ considerably in both route length and annual passenger numbers.
14. **P1-DAT-S-014**　Paris has 199 kilometres of track and serves approximately 1,191 million passengers annually.
15. **P1-DAT-S-015**　At 25%, A was approximately twice as high as B. / The figure for A, at 25%, was roughly double that for B.
16. **P1-DAT-S-016**　A overtook B and became the second-largest category by the end of the period.
17. **P1-DAT-S-017**　A recorded the highest figure of all the categories, whereas B remained well below the others.
18. **P1-DAT-S-018**　The figures for all four categories were around 20%, indicating a relatively even distribution.
19. **P1-DAT-S-019**　The chart shows the number of households rather than the proportions they represented.
20. **P1-DAT-S-020**　London has the longest network, extending for 394 kilometres, although it does not carry the largest number of passengers.

### 易错提醒

- `proportion / percentage / share` 用 `largest/smallest`；水平、价格或具体百分比常用 `highest/lowest`。
- `the number of households` 作主语时谓语用单数；`households` 作主语时谓语用复数。
- `twice as high as` 等于对方的两倍；不要使用含义容易歧义的 `two times higher than`。
- 人数用 `number/fewest`，不可数金额用 `amount/least`。

# 第五章　流程图：阶段、被动与因果

## 中译英表达答案

1. **P1-PRO-E-001**　consist of X main stages / involve X main stages
2. **P1-PRO-E-002**　begin with A and end with B / start with A and finish with B
3. **P1-PRO-E-003**　during this process / throughout the process
4. **P1-PRO-E-004**　Initially, ... / In the first stage, ...
5. **P1-PRO-E-005**　Subsequently, ... / Following this, ... / The next stage is ...
6. **P1-PRO-E-006**　Finally, ... / Eventually, ...
7. **P1-PRO-E-007**　lead to ... / result in ... / thereby producing ...
8. **P1-PRO-E-008**　unfold in sequence / occur in a fixed sequence
9. **P1-PRO-E-009**　be forced upwards / be pushed upwards
10. **P1-PRO-E-010**　give rise to ... / produce ... / form ...

## 中译英完整句答案

1. **P1-PRO-S-001**　Overall, the process consists of seven main stages, beginning with sea winds approaching the coast and ending with dry air reaching the inland area.
2. **P1-PRO-S-002**　Initially, moist air from the sea approaches the coast and is forced upwards when it encounters the windward slope of the mountains.
3. **P1-PRO-S-003**　As the moist air rises and cools, clouds form at higher altitudes.
4. **P1-PRO-S-004**　The air continues to rise, eventually resulting in rainfall near the mountain peak.
5. **P1-PRO-S-005**　After crossing the mountain range, the remaining dry air descends along the leeward slope.
6. **P1-PRO-S-006**　Because most of the moisture has already been lost, very little rainfall reaches the inland area.
7. **P1-PRO-S-007**　Consequently, a rain-shadow desert eventually forms on the inland side of the mountains.
8. **P1-PRO-S-008**　In the first stage, the raw materials are transported to the factory, where they are washed and sorted.
9. **P1-PRO-S-009**　The mixture is then heated until all the moisture has evaporated.
10. **P1-PRO-S-010**　This is a linear process in which the stages occur in a fixed sequence.

### 易错提醒

- 人工流程通常大量使用被动语态：`is transported / is heated / is packaged`。
- 自然过程可主动表达：`air rises`、`clouds form`、`rain falls`。
- `lead to / result in` 后接名词或动名词；`result from` 表示“源于”。
- 不要把所有步骤硬塞进一个长句；每句描述一至两个相邻阶段即可。
